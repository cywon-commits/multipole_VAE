**Figure S2. Decoder field profiles around masked blocks (Stage 2d A1).** Mean decoded $h_z$ of the unmasked sites as a function of the Chebyshev distance $d$ to the nearest masked site ($d=0$: masked sites) for the shared-$J$ (Stage 2b), M1 and M2 (Stage 2c) decoders ($\ell=8$, seed 0, 192 validation samples; (a) $\rho=0.25$, (b) $\rho=0.5$). Grey solid: $\beta B=2.4$; grey dashed: $h_m^*=\mathcal{L}^{-1}(\langle S_z\rangle_{\rm masked})$ (1.45--1.46). The unmasked plateau lies below 2.4 and is flat for $d\ge2$ (decoder receptive-field radius 3): the field deficit is a global level shift, not a boundary effect.
