**Figure S6. E1 latent direction versus field direction.** Distribution of $1-|\cos|$ between the used $l=1$ latent (posterior mean) and the true field direction $\hat n$ on 800 validation samples for the three decoders (colour; line style: seed 0, 1, 2; $L=32$, $J=5$, $\kappa=10$). The nine curves coincide: the latent direction equals the direction of each sample's magnetisation, which differs from $\hat n$ by the finite-size fluctuation ($\sim10^{-4}$ in $1-|\cos|$), so the curves do not discriminate between the decoders. Mean $|\cos|$: independent 0.99990, PL 0.99990, SM-alternating 0.99990 (minimum 0.9992; the float32 limit is $\sim10^{-4}$).
