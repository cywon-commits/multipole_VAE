**Figure 1. Model overview and test systems.** (a) Effective-field VAE: an SO(3)$_S$- and translation-equivariant encoder maps a spin configuration to a latent $z$ with $l=0$ and $l=1$ channels; the decoder returns the $l=1$ local effective field $h_i$. The independent decoder uses only $h_i$ (vMF observation model); the coupled decoder adds global nearest-neighbour bonds $J$ (and $D$, whose direction pattern is a fixed spurion) and is trained with the pseudo-likelihood. (b) Effective field on site $i$ with the Moriya-vector directions of the interfacial ($D_x=D\hat y$, $D_y=-D\hat x$) and bulk ($D_\mu=D\hat\mu$) models. (c) Texture examples (colour: $S_z$; arrows: in-plane components). Polarised state ($L=48$, $\langle S_z\rangle=0.963$, $Q=-0.00$), skyrmion lattice ($L=48$, $\langle S_z\rangle=0.428$, $Q=-32.0$; interfacial DMI $J=D=1$, $\beta=6$, annealed, seed 0) and one E1 sample ($L=32$, isotropic $J=5$, $\kappa=10$ along a random direction; $Q=-0.00$). Panels (a),(b) are schematic; no error bars.
