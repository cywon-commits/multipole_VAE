**Figure 5. The bias valley.** (a) E1 field-direction-noise bias of the coupled decoder: $\Delta J/J$ and $\Delta\kappa/\kappa$ versus the effective noise $s^2_{\rm eff}$ (per transverse component, $10^{-4}$). Solid lines: theory $+40.5\,s^2$ and $-91.0\,s^2$ ($L=32$); dotted: $L=64$ ($+40.5\,s^2$, $-91.0\,s^2$; the two lines nearly coincide, the $L$ dependence enters through $s^2_{\rm eff}$). Open black symbols: synthetic tests with independent noise ($10^{-4}$, $3\times10^{-4}$) and the $\hat M$ direction ($c=1$). Coloured symbols: VAE solutions (blue $L=32$; orange $L=64$; circles seed 0, triangles seed 1/2; filled $J$, open $\kappa$); for the three $\beta=1$, $L=32$ seeds $s^2_{\rm eff}$ is measured on 4000 samples ($s^2_{\rm eff}=$3.02, 2.82, 4.53$\times10^{-4}$), for the $\beta$-scan and $L=64$ points it is the theory value $3\beta/(2N\kappa_{\rm eff})-1/(N\kappa_0)$ ($\beta=0.25,0.5,1$). VAE $\Delta J/J$ at $\beta=1$: +1.49, +1.49, +1.49\%, at $L=64$: +0.29, +0.28\%. (b) Mask bias versus the field deficit $\delta=2.4-\langle h_z\rangle_{\rm unmasked}$ ($J$ blue, $D$ orange): shared-$J$ decoders (Stage 2b, open), M1 decoders (Stage 2c, filled circles) and continued K+M1 solutions (Stage 2e, triangles) against the $L=48$ valley lines $+4.7\%\,\delta$ and $-13.3\%\,\delta$. The shared-$J$ points lie above the line because a second mechanism acts in addition to the valley: masked sites that keep only part of their neighbours push $J$ up directly (the misspecified factorised masked likelihood). (c) Separate field for the masked sites removes the bias: biases of the 16 runs each of M1-hm and M2-hm (2 seeds; $\ell=2,8$; $\rho=0.25,0.5$; max $|\Delta J/J|=$0.20\%, max $|\Delta D/D|=$0.37\%) against 16 shared-field M1 runs (Stage 2c); grey band: $\pm0.5\%$. (d) Cross-section of the unmasked pseudo-likelihood along the valley ($L=48$, 320 samples, one seed): cost of a field deficit $\delta$ with $J,D$ re-fitted or fixed; inset: valley slopes per unit deficit (4.61--4.80\% for $J$, -13.45---13.33\% for $D$). No error bars (seed values are shown individually).
