**Figure 6. Collapse of the latent.** (a) KL versus $\beta$ for the E2 independent decoder (two seeds: squares/light, triangles/dark; log--log axes; KL $=0$ points are drawn on the floor, marked by down-triangles): training from scratch with a 20-epoch warm-up (open) collapses for $\beta\ge0.3$ (and for seed 1 at $\beta=0.1$), while continued training from the $\beta=0.01$ solution keeps KL $>0$ up to $\beta=10$ (seed 0/1: 92.5/67.2 nat). A single $\times3.16$ step to $\beta=31.6$ collapses both seeds; the fine grid ($\times1.26$ steps, black-edged) is smooth. Dotted verticals: tangent lower bounds $\beta_{\rm lb}=38.5$ (seed 0), $35.0$ (seed 1). Stars/crosses: B-2 (fixed $\beta=31.75$, learning rate/3 from the $\beta=25.2$ state) and flat start from $\beta=10$. (b) Linear zoom, $\beta=8$--40: the seed-1 fine-grid states ($n=4$) are fitted by a straight line (slope $-2.4$ nat per unit $\beta$) with zero at $\beta\approx28.6$ (the zero depends on the window: 26.2 including the $\beta=10$ state, 34.2 when the last chain states and the B-2 state are used; the curve is slightly convex and the B-2 state still has KL $=3.8$ nat at $\beta=31.75$); seed 0 is still at KL $=13.7$ nat at $\beta=31.75$. (c) Tangent construction in the $(R,D)$ plane (expected reconstruction with posterior-sampled $z$; $D_c=5155.3$ of the collapsed solution, star at $R=0$): the fine-grid chain (line; numbers: $(D_c-D)/R$) ends on the tangent of slope $-\beta_{\rm lb}$ (dotted); flat-start (crosses) and B-2 (stars) states are different solutions at the same $\beta$ and are not connected. Insets: full range. (d) Periodic-phase toy model (one mode, uniform prior over a period, bounded gain): first-order transition with KL jumping from 0.95 nat to zero at $\beta/{\rm SNR}=0.092$ (dashed; dotted: metastable branch) versus continuous Gaussian water-filling; this first-order behaviour holds only in this restricted (Gaussian-posterior) parametrisation. Error bars: none; seed values are shown individually.
