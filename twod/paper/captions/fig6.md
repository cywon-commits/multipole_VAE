**Figure 6. Collapse of the latent.** (a) KL versus $\beta$ for the E2 independent decoder (two seeds: squares/light, triangles/dark; log--log axes; KL $=0$ points are drawn on the floor, marked by down-triangles): training from scratch with a 20-epoch warm-up (open) collapses for $\beta\ge0.3$ (and for seed 1 at $\beta=0.1$), while continued training from the $\beta=0.01$ solution keeps KL $>0$ up to $\beta=10$ (seed 0/1: 92.5/67.2 nat). A single $\times3.16$ step to $\beta=31.6$ collapses both seeds; the fine grid ($\times1.26$ steps, black-edged) is smooth. Dotted verticals: tangent lower bounds $\beta_{\rm lb}=38.5$ (seed 0), $35.0$ (seed 1). Stars/crosses: B-2 (fixed $\beta=31.75$, learning rate/3 from the $\beta=25.2$ state) and flat start from $\beta=10$. (b) Linear zoom, $\beta=8$--40: fine-grid, continued and flat-start states of both seeds, with the B-2 state (large star: KL $=3.8$ nat at $\beta=31.75$) still carrying information at the last grid point; seed 0 is at KL $=13.7$ nat at $\beta=31.75$. The curve is convex near the end, so a linear extrapolation of the fine grid is not a transition estimate (its zero depends on the fit window; see README). (c) Tangent construction in the $(R,D)$ plane (expected reconstruction with posterior-sampled $z$; $D_c=5155.3$ of the collapsed solution, star at $R=0$): the fine-grid chain (line; numbers: $(D_c-D)/R$) ends on the tangent of slope $-\beta_{\rm lb}$ (dotted); the state through which the tangent passes (red diamond, maximal $(D_c-D)/R$) is, for seed 0, the chain state at $\beta=31.76$ (38.46; the flat-start state at the same $\beta$ gives 35.23, so it is not the flat start) and, for seed 1, the B-2 state at $\beta=31.75$ (34.95; the last chain state gives 30.97); flat-start (crosses) and B-2 (stars) states are different solutions at the same $\beta$ and are not connected. Insets: full range. (d) Periodic-phase toy model (one mode, uniform prior over a period, bounded gain): first-order transition with KL jumping from 0.95 nat to zero at $\beta/{\rm SNR}=0.092$ (dashed; dotted: metastable branch) versus continuous Gaussian water-filling; this first-order behaviour holds only in this restricted (Gaussian-posterior) parametrisation. Error bars: none; seed values are shown individually.
